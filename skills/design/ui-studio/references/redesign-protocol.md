# Redesign protocol (live products)

Moved here from SKILL.md to keep SKILL.md under its line cap.
Use it in Audit / Redesign mode, after step 6's audit and before any build. Conflict numbers refer to
SKILL.md "When members disagree".

- **Audit first:** brand tokens, IA and conversion paths, content blocks doing work versus filler,
  patterns to keep and to retire, the current dial reading, the SEO baseline [v2 §11.B]; walk every
  screen from mobile to desktop as a user [design-audit].
- **Levers in order, stop when the brief is met:** typography, spacing and rhythm, colour recalibration,
  motion layer, hero and key-section recomposition, full block replacement. Sound IA, content and SEO
  means targeted evolution with the first four levers [v2 §11.D-E].
- **Never change silently:** URLs and slugs, nav labels, form field names and order, analytics event
  hooks and section IDs, the logo, legal and consent copy [v2 §11.F]. Function stays exactly as it was;
  a design idea that needs a functional change is flagged, not built [design-audit scope].
- **Reduction filter** on every element: remove it if nothing is lost; redesign it until nobody needs to
  be told it exists; visual weight follows functional importance [design-audit].
- **Approval:** the plan goes to the user as a review page before any build; then one phase at a time with
  before/after shots, and progress and lessons noted afterwards [design-audit, House rules].
- **Sourced replacements:** when a block is replaced with a sourced component (Watermelon, Skiper UI),
  its card on the user's review page names the source, licence, new dependencies and what was restyled
  (`component-sources.md`).
