# Anti-slop tells (merged)

What makes a page read as machine-made, merged from nine members (impeccable's craft-floor Refuse list
included), each with the move that replaces it.
One tell proves little; clusters and repetition are the signal [taste-skill copy-tells]. A ban without a
positive move breeds the nearest substitute, so every line names what to do instead. A brief, a brand
asset or the user's own words override any line here; say so in the plan.

## Color and surface

- AI purple, violet, lavender, purple-blue glows, neon gradients by reflex. Instead: a neutral base plus
  one accent chosen for the trade (emerald, cobalt, deep rose, burnt orange, terracotta). A brand that is
  already purple stays purple, executed with intent [taste-skill, v1, v2 §4.2].
- Default SaaS blue `#3B82F6` with no reason [bencium-controlled, bencium-impact].
- The premium-consumer default: cream/bone grounds (`#f5f1ea #f7f5f1 #fbf8f1 #efeae0 #faf7f1 #e8dfcb`),
  brass/clay/oxblood/ochre accents (`#b08947 #b6553a #9a2436 #9c6e2a #bc7c3a`), espresso text
  (`#1a1714`). Instead rotate: cold luxury (silver, chrome, smoke), forest (deep green, bone, amber),
  black and tan, cobalt and cream, terracotta and slate, olive and brick on paper, or monochrome plus one
  saturated pop. Never the same family twice in a row [v2 §4.2].
- Pure `#000000` or `#ffffff` on large surfaces. Instead off-black (zinc-950, `#0A0A0A`-`#121212`) and
  off-white [v1, v2, brutalist].
- Warm and cool greys mixed in one project; a second accent appearing in section 7 [v1, v2 color lock].
- Outer glows, Tailwind `shadow-md/lg/xl` defaults, pure-black drop shadows on light grounds. Instead
  layered low-opacity shadows tinted to the ground, or a 1px inner border [v1, minimalist, soft].
- Gradient-filled text on any heading. Emphasis comes from weight or size [v1, stitch, impeccable].
- Colour scattered as small accents over a grey page when the direction needs a field that owns a
  region; light or dark picked by category instead of by the use scene (who, where, what light)
  [impeccable new-work].
- Glass on everything. Glass belongs to glass directions only, blur on fixed layers only [bencium, v2 §5,
  impeccable].
- Hard offset shadows (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0`) outside a world that really is neobrutalist; zero-offset
  coloured halos posing as depth. Shadows carry an offset and a soft blur [impeccable craft floor].
- A coloured `border-left` or `border-right` over 1px on cards, list items, callouts or alerts [impeccable].
- A hero whose only visual is text plus a gradient blob. Instead a real visual: product, photo, live UI [v2 §4.8].
- Custom mouse cursors (outdated, hostile to accessibility and performance) [v1, stitch, v2].
- A page whose theme flips mid-scroll (one cream section inside a dark page) [v2 §4.11].
- Mixed radius systems (pill buttons on a square-card page) with no written rule [v2 shape lock].

## Typography

- Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans or Space Grotesk as the brand face by reflex; Fraunces or Instrument
  Serif as the reflex display serif; "creative brief, so serif". Instead a face chosen for the brand,
  rotated across projects [bencium-impact, v1, v2 §4.1]. Internal tools and public-sector pages may use
  Inter or system fonts for legibility [v2 override]. On Persuade and Experience pages these are reflex
  picks too unless a reason no other face meets is written down (a subject association is not a reason):
  Playfair Display, Cormorant, Lora, Crimson, Newsreader, Syne, Space Mono, IBM Plex, DM Sans, DM Serif,
  Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Sans, Inter as a display face [impeccable new-work].
- A system display face (Impact, Arial Black, the platform sans) as the display voice of an own-world
  page; source and self-host a face with the right character [impeccable craft floor].
- Monospace as a costume for "technical"; mono is for code, data and measurements [impeccable].
- A screaming H1, or a hero headline of 4+ lines (a font-size error, not a copy-length error) [v1, v2 §4.7].
- One serif word injected into a sans headline for emphasis. Instead italic or bold of the same family [v2].
- `<br>`-split, italicised headlines as the default move [v2 §9.F].
- Straight quotes, `--` for dashes, three periods for an ellipsis, (c) for the copyright sign [typography].
- Body text in pure black, or grey on grey under 4.5:1 [minimalist, ui-ux-pro-max].

## Layout

- Centered hero over a dark mesh; three equal feature cards; endless left/right zigzag (cap: 2 in a row)
  [v1, v2 §4.7]. Centered is fine for manifesto or launch pages where the message is the design.
- A bento with empty cells, or six white-on-white text cards. Instead N items = N cells and 2-3 cells with
  real visual variation (photo, pattern, tinted ground) [v2, gpt-taste].
- Split header: big headline left, a small explainer floating top-right. Stack them instead [v2].
- Same-size cards of icon plus heading plus text as the page structure; nested cards (always wrong); the
  hero-metric template (big number, small label, supporting stats, accent); sparklines, progress rings
  and soft rounded rectangles standing in for content; a modal for a task that needs neither interruption
  nor protected focus [impeccable craft floor].
- Eyebrows (kickers above headings). None by default; only a named preset or the user's brief brings them
  back, capped at one per three sections, the hero counts [impeccable, v2]. Section numbers
  ("001 · Capabilities", "00 / INDEX"), "01 / 4" pagination on tiles, meta labels ("SECTION 01",
  "QUESTION 05", "ABOUT US") [gpt-taste, v2 §9.F].
- Hero clutter: version labels (V0.6, BETA), "Brand · No. 01" sub-eyebrows, trust micro-strips, pricing
  teasers, avatar rows, pill tags or floating badges on the headline, a decoration strip at the hero
  bottom ("BRAND. MOTION. SPATIAL."). Move proof into its own section under the hero [gpt-taste, v2].
- Scroll cues ("Scroll to explore", bouncing chevrons, mouse-wheel icons) [stitch, v2].
- Decorative status dots, crosshair or hairline grids drawn only to look designed, vertical rotated text,
  locale/time/weather strips [v2]. The brutalist preset uses crosshairs on purpose; that is a direction,
  not a reflex.
- A spec table with a hairline under every row. Instead grouped chunks, 2-column spec cards, or 3-4
  featured specs plus a "View full specifications" disclosure [v2 §4.9].
- Filled-track score bars as comparison visuals on a marketing page [v2].
- A logo wall inside the hero, plain-text wordmarks, category labels under logos. Logos only, under the
  hero, legible in both themes [v2 §4.8].
- Pills or labels laid over photos; captions under every image; decorative photo credits. Credits go in
  one footer line [taste-skill rule 1, v2].
- Popups, newsletter modals, chat bubbles, help pills, back-to-top squares, award strips [taste-skill layout-v4].
- A two-line desktop nav, or a nav taller than 80px [v2].

## Content and data

- John Doe, Sarah Chan, Acme, Nexus, SmartFlow, Cloudly; egg avatars. Instead realistic local names and
  real or stand-in photos [v1, v2, minimalist].
- Fake-perfect numbers (99.99%, 50%, 1234567) and invented precise specs (4.1x, 5.8 mm, 13.4 lb).
  Instead real data from the brief, or a visibly labelled mock [v1, v2 §4.9].
- Invented reviews, quotes, customers, figures or logos; a drawn look-alike of a real logo [taste-skill §6].
- Div-built fake screenshots, dashboards or terminals; fake version footers ("v1.4.2", "last sync 4s ago").
  Instead the product's real UI as live HTML, a real screenshot, or a photograph [v2, taste-skill].
- Emoji or Unicode glyphs as icons, or emoji inside professional UI copy [v1, ui-ux-pro-max, typography,
  impeccable]. Allowed only when the user asks for a chat or social-native voice [v2 override].
- Circles, polygons or radial-gradient cutouts faking a photo subject's organic edge. Use a real alpha
  matte or a cut-out asset, or leave the shape out [impeccable craft floor].
- Two CTAs with the same intent on one page ("Get in touch" + "Let's talk"). One label per intent, used
  in nav, hero and footer alike [v2 §4.5].

## Copy words and shapes

- Words that stand in for a fact: delve, tapestry, testament to, realm, embark, beacon, multifaceted,
  paradigm, myriad, plethora, meticulous, intricate, elevate, unlock, unleash, empower, harness,
  supercharge, seamless, effortless, cutting-edge, revolutionary, transformative, holistic, next-gen,
  world-class, best-in-class, game-changer, state-of-the-art, synergy, leverage, reimagine, powerful,
  robust, nestled, unparalleled. Write the fact the word replaces: "seamless" becomes the setup steps,
  "robust" the failure case, "powerful" the figure. Literal trade uses stay (a climbing harness, bespoke
  tailoring, an elevated terrace) [taste-skill copy-tells, minimalist, v1].
- Stock lines: "In today's fast-paced world", "Unlock the power of", "The future of X is here", "Say
  goodbye to", "Whether you're A or B", "Join thousands of", "Trusted by teams worldwide", "Ready to
  transform your X?", "Look no further", "X made simple", "at your fingertips", "It's not just X, it's Y"
  [taste-skill].
- Shapes: the -ing rider (", ensuring a smooth start"), the answered question ("The result? Three hours
  back."), the colon reveal, "serves as / stands as" where "is" works [taste-skill].
- Em dash as a pause, anywhere in new copy. Restructure: a period, a comma, parentheses [v2 §9.G].
- Performative-craftsman labels ("Field notes", "Currently on the bench", "Quietly trusted by"),
  micro-meta sentences under headings, generic step labels ("Stage 1", "Phase 01"). Use plain labels or
  the step's own verb ("Install", "Configure", "Ship") [v2 §9.F].
- Residue: "Certainly!", "I hope this helps", [Company Name], lorem ipsum, `{{tokens}}`, markdown
  asterisks on the rendered page [taste-skill].
- Over-correction: once the old words are gone, models reach for new tics (two-fragment headings, virtue
  words, contrast frames). The cure is the specific fact from the brief, not a synonym [taste-skill].

## Motion

- Every card or section fading in as you scroll; parallax on text; scroll-jacking; random blinking
  [taste-skill §6].
- Infinite loops on informational cards; two or more marquees on one page [v2 §5].
- `transition: all`, linear easing on UI, instant 0ms state snaps [make-interfaces, soft, ui-ux-pro-max].
- GSAP everywhere because it is installed; `window.addEventListener('scroll')` [v2 §5.D].

## Calibration (AI default looks) [impeccable new-work]

Generated interfaces cluster around a few looks whatever the subject: warm cream ground, high-contrast
serif display and a terracotta or signal-red accent; near-black with one neon accent and glowing edges;
broadsheet hairlines, italic display serif and small tracked mono labels; flat saturated ink with black
boxes, hard offset shadows and heavy condensed type. Each is fine when the brief asks for it. When the
aesthetic was free and the page lands in one, or anyone could guess the look from the category alone,
rework from the subject's own materials. Warm, bookish, family and child-facing subjects pull hardest
toward cream plus serif: treat that first palette as spent.
